## 📌 About the Internship

The simulation focused on building a strong practical foundation in **data analytics** and **forensic technology**, applied to real-world consulting problems for a hypothetical client, Daikibo Industrials — a global machinery manufacturer. Tasks included unifying multi-source telemetry data, building an interactive dashboard, and conducting a forensic investigation into workplace pay equity, working through a realistic business brief in the way a Deloitte data analyst would.

**Organization:** Deloitte, one of the world's leading "Big Four" professional services networks — delivered via **Forage**, a free, open-access virtual work-experience platform used by students worldwide.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Clean, unify, and prepare real-world business datasets for analysis
- Consolidate telemetry data from multiple factory sites (single JSON file) into one dataset
- Identify operational bottlenecks and downtime trends
- Design an interactive dashboard in Tableau to communicate insights to stakeholders
- Apply a forensic algorithm in Excel to quantify gender pay equality across roles and locations
- Classify complex datasets and identify where pay gaps were most prevalent
- Propose clear, evidence-based business conclusions to a forensic lead
